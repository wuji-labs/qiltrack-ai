## What is Qiltrack AI?

Qiltrack AI transforms complex financial data into clear, actionable research reports in 30 seconds. We democratize institutional-grade equity research through machine intelligence.

## Features

- **5 Research Styles** — Bull, Bear, Balanced, Technical, and Deep-Dive perspectives
- **30-Second Reports** — AI-generated comprehensive analysis
- **13 Languages** — English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, العربية, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Bahasa Melayu, Nederlands, Русский
- **Global Coverage** — US, HK, CN, TW markets
- **Evidence Tracing** — Every claim linked to original SEC filings or news sources
- **Pro Export Suite** — PDF, Word, and clipboard export
- **Daily Alpha Inbox** — Top market insights delivered to your email

## How It Works

1. Enter a stock ticker (e.g., AAPL, TSLA, 0700.HK)
2. Choose your research style and analysis persona
3. Get AI-generated report in 30 seconds
4. Export to PDF/DOCX or share
